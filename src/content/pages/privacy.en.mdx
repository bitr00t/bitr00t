## Principle

This site is built to process as little as possible. There are no user
accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. Fonts and scripts
are served from this server; nothing is loaded from Google Fonts or a CDN.

## Server logs

The host automatically records the usual access data: the address
requested, date and time, volume transferred, browser and operating
system, referrer, and IP address. This data is not combined with any other
source.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — a legitimate interest in operating
the site securely and correctly. Logs are deleted after
[RETENTION PERIOD]. Hosting is provided by [HOST AND ADDRESS], under a
data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR.

## Local storage

This site sets no cookies.

Switching between light and dark appearance stores that choice in your
browser's `localStorage` under the key `theme`. The value is never sent to
the server and contains only `light` or `dark`. Because it is strictly
necessary for a function you explicitly requested, no consent is required
(§ 25(2)(2) TDDDG). You can clear it through your browser settings.

## Search

Search runs entirely in your browser. Opening it fetches a file of article
titles and text from this server. Your search terms never leave your device
and are not logged.

## Embedded video

Articles may embed video from YouTube or Vimeo, using the privacy-enhanced
mode. Playing a video connects to the provider's servers and transmits your
IP address. Providers are Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow
Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and Vimeo.com, Inc., 330 West 34th Street, New
York, NY 10001, USA. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
